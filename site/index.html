    03How it works

    Learned perception, explainable rules

    Models find and follow road users; rules decide what counts as a violation, so every event can be explained.

    1. 1

      Align

      Every 3rd frame is decoded; the camera mount drifts, so each recording is re-aligned to one reference frame every 10 s.

      classical
    2. 2

      Perceive

      YOLO11n finds cars, buses and pedestrians; BoT-SORT follows them; the signal colour is read from its lamp.

      learned
    3. 3

      Reason

      Tracks are converted to metres and checked against 14 rules: stop line, zebras, lanes, signal state.

      rule-based
    4. 4

      Verify

      Rare, high-stakes events (accident, near-miss, obstacle) are confirmed by the Qwen3-VL-2B vision-language model.

      learned
    Full pipeline diagram and component table
    ComponentHow
    Detection & trackingLearned: YOLO11n (COCO) + BoT-SORT
    Camera alignmentClassical: ORB features, RANSAC similarity, every 10 s
    Metric calibrationFitted pinhole camera (pedestrian heights, lane vanishing point, 14 m stop line)
    Traffic-light colourClassical: HSV + lamp position
    14 event classesRules on trajectories, geometry and light state
    Accident / near-miss / obstacleLearned: Qwen3-VL-2B verifier, P(yes) from next-token logits
    Accident risk (Part B)Physics: time-to-collision and required deceleration between tracks

    05Report

    What worked, what did not, what is next

    ✓What worked

    • Checking every rule on real frames caught the pedestrian-vs-vehicle signal mix-up, riders counted as pedestrians, people waiting on the kerb, and red-to-green line creeping.
    • Traffic-light state from lit-pixel count plus lamp position: clean ~33 s red / ~36 s green cycles on all four videos, day and dusk.
    • Runtime alignment: the same geometry lands on the zebras in every recording.
    • Metric calibration from pedestrian heights: two independent scale cues agree within 3 %.
    • The Qwen3-VL-2B verifier accepted 0 of 98 false accident / near-miss / obstacle candidates.
    • 1.6–1.7× video duration for Part A + B, inside the 3× limit.

    ✗What did not (yet)

    • No ground truth: thresholds are tuned by eye, so precision and recall are not measured.
    • The verifier's recall is untested: the samples contain no accident. Fire/smoke stays disabled.
    • The tracker switches IDs at 10 fps, which shortens trajectories.
    • Not yet timed on the evaluation GPU (T4).
    • Part B is a physics baseline without accident examples to calibrate on.

    →Next

    • Annotate the sample videos and tune thresholds against evaluate.py.
    • Model the far-road approach (stop line, signal from traffic flow).
